# Support — Android TV Data Limiter

NOEMA includes built-in diagnostics so Android TV / Google TV problems can be reported without ADB.

## Best way to report a problem

Open **Diagnostics** or **Report problem** inside NOEMA and include as much of the following as possible:

- device manufacturer and model
- Android / Google TV version
- NOEMA version
- selected bandwidth profile
- streaming app or service affected
- what happened
- whether the problem persists after Wi-Fi reconnect or device restart

The app can prepare a diagnostic support report locally. Nothing is sent automatically.

If the TV has a compatible email/share app, choose **Send report**. Otherwise use **Copy report** or send a photo/screenshot of the diagnostic screen.
